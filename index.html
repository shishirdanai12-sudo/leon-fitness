Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining Leon Fitness.

What are your membership prices?
Monthly Pass: Rs 5,000/month  |  Quarterly Pass: Rs 12,000/3 months  |  Annual Pass: Rs 45,000/year. All plans include unlimited gym access, sports facility access, and personal training sessions.
Do you offer a free trial?
Yes! Your first personal training consultation is completely free. Book a session through our booking page or call us at 9767474554.
What are your operating hours?
Mon–Fri: 5AM – 10PM  |  Sat–Sun: 7AM – 9PM. Members enjoy 24/7 access.
Is the sauna included in membership?
Sauna sessions are Rs 1,000 per session for groups of 5+. Not included in standard membership, but discounted for members.
Can I cancel my membership anytime?
Yes. Memberships can be cancelled with 7 days' notice. No long-term lock-in contracts required.
Do you have Zumba classes?
Yes, every morning at 5AM in Studio A. High-energy Latin dance fitness with a certified instructor. All levels welcome.
